# Design tokens

Source: `@waslu/ui/tailwind-preset` and `@waslu/ui/tokens`.

## Colors
- `brand` 50-900
- `neutral` 50-900
- `success` 100/700
- `warning` 100/700
- `danger` 100/700

## Typography
- `text-xs`, `text-sm`, `text-base`, `text-lg`, `text-xl`, `text-2xl`

## Spacing
- `1,2,3,4,5,6,8,10`

## Radius
- `rounded-sm`, `rounded-md`, `rounded-lg`, `rounded-xl`

## Shadows
- `shadow-sm`, `shadow-md`, `shadow-lg`

## Breakpoints
- `xs` = 360px
- `sm`, `md`, `lg`, `xl`

## WCAG guidance
Use high-contrast defaults:
- `text-neutral-900` on `bg-white`
- `text-white` on `bg-brand-600`
- `text-danger-700` on `bg-danger-100`
